About ACM

The R.V.R & J.C. ACM Student Chapter, The Forerunners (160342) is the official student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery, established at RVR & JC College of Engineering by the Nagarjuna Education Society in 1985.

Guided by the motto Knowledge Is Power, the chapter connects students to a global network of researchers and professionals, offering workshops, coding contests, hackathons, and collaborative projects that transform curiosity into real-world impact.

As an affiliate of ACM — the world's largest educational and scientific computing society members gain access to cutting-edge resources, mentorship, and opportunities to compete at local, regional, and global levels.

Our Vision

To build a thriving ecosystem where students evolve into skilled computing professionals — driven by curiosity, collaboration, and a commitment to solving real-world challenges through technology.

To bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practice by fostering hands-on experiences, research exposure, and participation in national and global computing communities.

To inspire a lifelong passion for learning and innovation that extends well beyond the classroom, empowering every member to contribute meaningfully to the field of computing.

Our Mission

To provide students with a platform to explore, learn, and excel in diverse domains of computer science and emerging technologies through workshops, hackathons, and hands-on projects.

To connect students with the global ACM community, enabling access to valuable resources, mentorship, and networking opportunities with industry leaders and academicians.

To encourage active participation in local, regional, and international competitions, empowering members to showcase, refine, and validate their skills on wider platforms.
